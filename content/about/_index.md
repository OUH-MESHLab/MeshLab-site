> **Software that makes precision care possible.**
>
> *Clinicians, researchers and patients connected through our software.*

MESH|Lab is the medical-software research group hosted at **Oslo
University Hospital (OUS)**. We translate
clinical needs — our own and our collaborators' — into practical,
production-grade software for precision medicine.

## What we work on

We span three layers, from infrastructure up to the bedside:

1. **Operating systems and platforms** for reproducible medical
   computing — currently centred on [SystoleOS](/projects/systoleos/).
2. **Infrastructure for clinical and research data** — interoperability
   with bedside devices, image archives, and surgical-navigation
   systems.
3. **End-user medical applications** — 3D Slicer extensions for
   surgical planning and intra-operative guidance, simulation, and
   image analysis.

Across these layers we organise the work along four research themes:
[Systems & infrastructure](/research/systems-infrastructure/),
[Image-guided surgery & navigation](/research/image-guided-surgery/),
[Surgical simulation & robotics](/research/simulation-robotics/), and
[Clinical data & AI](/research/data-ai/).

## How we work

Six principles shape day-to-day decisions and project selection:

- **Needs-driven.** We continuously map unmet clinical problems and
  rank them by impact and feasibility. The research roadmap follows
  the clinical roadmap.
- **Interdisciplinary by default.** Clinicians, data scientists,
  software engineers, and regulatory experts work together on every
  project — not in series.
- **Rigorous and compliant.** We build under modern agile practice
  *and* the standards medical software is held to: IEC 62304,
  ISO 13485, GDPR, and FAIR data principles.
- **Sustainably funded.** We diversify income across competitive
  grants, industry co-development, service contracts, and spin-outs.
- **Open where we can.** Most of our software releases are
  open-source; we maintain them long-term, not just at paper-publish
  time. See the
  [OUH-MESHLab GitHub organisation](https://github.com/OUH-MESHLab).
- **Measured.** We track adoption — downloads, citations, deployed
  installations — and let those numbers reshape priorities.

## Where we sit

MESH|Lab is **decentralised by design**. The lab functions as a
network rather than a hierarchy: a small core at OUS coordinates
with associate members in clinical and technical roles, visiting
researchers from partner institutions, and external clinical
consultants in domains we don't carry in-house.

Projects live on
[GitHub](https://github.com/OUH-MESHLab) — *if it's not registered, it
does not exist.* Coordination happens through one monthly all-hands
sync (one topic per meeting) plus focused project meetings as needed.

## Our partners

We sustain active collaborations with hospitals, universities,
research institutes, and industry across Europe and North America.
The current network includes Brigham and Women's Hospital (Harvard),
Inria, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, the Paris Brain Institute,
Queen's University, Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, and NVIDIA.

See the [Projects](/projects/) and [Team](/team/) pages for who's
working on what — and [Join us](/join/) if you want to do precision-
medicine software for real patients, with the regulatory rigour the
clinic demands.
