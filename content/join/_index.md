MESH|Lab takes on collaborators in three shapes: **PhD candidates**,
**MSc projects**, and **engineering / clinical-fellow positions**
tied to specific funded projects.

## Currently open

*No formally posted positions at the moment.*

We do open positions in response to funded calls (NIH-equivalent
grants, MSCA fellowships, EU networks) — when one opens, it is
posted on the [OUS career portal](https://www.ous-research.no/),
and we link it here.

## Self-initiated proposals

If your background fits one of the [research themes](/research/) and
you would like to propose a project, write to
[rafael.palomar@ous-research.no](mailto:rafael.palomar@ous-research.no)
with:

- a one-page sketch of the project you have in mind,
- your CV,
- and a sentence on why MESH|Lab is the right host (we look for a
  concrete clinical or methodological connection, not generic fit).

MSc students at OUS-affiliated programmes can reach out directly with
a thesis topic; visiting researchers from partner institutions
([see Projects](/projects/) for the active partnerships) are welcome
to discuss a stay.

## What we look for

- Engineering rigour. Most of our work ends up running near patients;
  we cannot afford to ship code that has not been tested against the
  workflows it will be used in.
- Clinical curiosity. Even on the engineering side of the lab, we
  expect everyone to understand the clinical problem they are
  working on — not just the API.
- Open-source instinct. Most of our software releases are open-source
  and we maintain them after publication.
