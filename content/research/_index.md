Our work sits at the intersection of clinical practice and software
engineering. We organise it along four research themes — every project
on the [Projects page](/projects/) belongs to one or more of these.

The themes are not silos. A surgical-planning project may rest on the
same operating-system platform we build for monitoring devices, and a
robotics simulation may feed training data into a clinical-AI pipeline.
The themes describe *what kind of problem* a project addresses; the
team and partner relationships cross-cut.
